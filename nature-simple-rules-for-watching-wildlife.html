Simple Rules for Watching Wildlife

Wildlife watching can be rewarding without getting too close. A calm approach and respect for animals can make outdoor encounters better for everyone.

Give Animals Plenty of Space

The most important rule is to avoid getting too close. If an animal changes its behaviour because you are nearby, increase the distance and allow it to continue undisturbed.

Watching from a comfortable distance often gives you a better opportunity to see natural behaviour anyway.

Stay on Appropriate Paths

When exploring outdoor spaces, use established paths and follow local signs or guidance. This helps protect plants, habitats, and areas where animals may be resting or feeding.

Respecting access rules is an important part of responsible outdoor exploration.

Keep Noise Low

Loud voices and sudden sounds can disturb wildlife and make it harder for you to observe anything naturally. Move calmly and keep conversations at a considerate volume.

Quiet observation can also make the experience more enjoyable because you become more aware of smaller movements and sounds.

Do Not Feed Wild Animals

Feeding wildlife can change natural behaviour and may create problems for animals and other visitors. It is better to observe without trying to attract animals closer.

Allow wildlife to remain independent and behave naturally.

Leave the Environment as You Found It

Take belongings and rubbish home with you, avoid damaging plants, and leave natural objects where they are.

Responsible wildlife watching is ultimately about observing rather than interfering. Give animals space, respect the surroundings, and let the encounter happen naturally.
