Small Things to Look For in a Garden

A garden can offer much more to notice than its larger plants and flowers. Slow down and small details begin to stand out.

Look Closely at Leaves

Leaves come in a wide variety of shapes, colours, textures, and patterns. Look at how they change as they grow, respond to weather, or catch the light at different times of day.

Even familiar plants can reveal details that are easy to miss when viewed from a distance.

Watch for Insects

Small insects can bring a surprising amount of activity to a garden. Watch carefully around flowers, leaves, soil, and sheltered areas without disturbing what you find.

You may notice movement that is easy to overlook when simply walking through the space.

Listen to the Garden

Gardens have their own collection of sounds. Birds, insects, leaves, wind, and rain can all change the atmosphere of the same space.

Taking a few quiet moments to listen can make the garden feel more active and interesting.

Notice Texture and Shape

Bark, stones, soil, petals, leaves, and stems all have different textures. Look at the contrast between rough and smooth surfaces or the different shapes created by plants and natural objects.

These details can be especially noticeable when you take time to look rather than simply pass through.

Watch How the Space Changes

A garden is always changing. New growth appears, flowers open and fade, leaves move with the weather, and different creatures become active at different times.

You do not need specialist knowledge to enjoy these changes. Curiosity and attention are enough to discover more in a space you may already know well.
